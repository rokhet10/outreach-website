Our Story

We believe AI education
should be free.

AI will change everything around us. The question is—are we ready?

The idea for CodeReach began when Rohan first discovered programming through Scratch in elementary school. What started with simple games and animations gradually grew into an interest in Python, machine learning, and AI. What began as curiosity slowly became something he genuinely loves building and learning about every day. Along the way, he realized how much early exposure can shape a student’s confidence and curiosity. Sometimes, all it takes is one project for coding to become exciting instead of intimidating.

That’s why we created CodeReach: a free, student-led initiative that makes computer science and AI approachable, hands-on, and accessible to everyone.

We believe students learn best by building. Instead of long lectures, we focus on real projects, practical skills, and creating an environment where it's okay to ask questions, make mistakes, and learn together. Because we're students too, we understand what it's like to be just getting started—and we're always happy to help.

Whether you've never written a line of code or you're simply curious about AI, you belong here.

No gatekeeping. No experience needed. Just curiosity.

From the Field

What we've been up to

June 2026

Summer Camp — Learning Python and AI

Our first summer camp kicked off in Northville, bringing together students with a wide range of coding experience. By the end of the camp, they had written Python programs and ending with a ML project to predict breast cancer using AI models and a published dataset.

Summer 2026

Growing Beyond Northville

We inspired students in different states and offered free curriculum so they could learn to code and teach others.
