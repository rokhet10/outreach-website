Our Story

We believe AI education
should be free.

CodeReach is a student-led initiative making world-class CS and AI education accessible to everyone — regardless of background or resources.

Mission

Closing the AI literacy gap

AI is about to change everything. The question is—are you ready?

We’re CodeReach, a student-led initiative introducing you to a world of possibilities. Through free workshops, live Zoom sessions, and real projects, we help you go from curious to capable.

No gatekeeping. No experience needed. Just curiosity.

Every session is recorded. Everything is open. If you’re ready to explore what AI can do—you’re in.
