About CodeReach

CodeReach is a free, student-led educational organization based in Northville, Michigan. We provide AI and computer science education to middle and high school students through live workshops, online sessions, and a free summer program — at no cost to students.

We are not a commercial entity. We do not charge for any programs or services.
