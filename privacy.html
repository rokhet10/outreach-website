Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

About CodeReach

CodeReach is a free, student-led educational organization dedicated to making AI and computer science education accessible to all students. We do not charge for any services, sell products, or process payments.

Information We Collect

We collect limited, anonymized data solely to understand how visitors use our site and improve our educational content:

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data such as page views, session duration, and general geographic region. This data does not identify individual users.
  • Contact form / email: If you email us directly, we receive your email address and message solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Sign-up forms: If you register for a workshop or summer camp, we collect your name and email to send program information. This data is never sold or shared with third parties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To communicate program details, schedules, and updates to registered students
  • To improve our website and educational content
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third parties

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Children's Privacy

CodeReach serves middle and high school students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is necessary to enroll in a free program. Parents or guardians may contact us at any time to request deletion of their child's information.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect any information submitted to us. Our website is served over HTTPS and we do not store payment information of any kind.
