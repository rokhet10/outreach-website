Hi, I'm Rohan — a freshman at Northville High School usually balancing life between sports fields and academic pursuits.

When I'm not pitching on the baseball diamond or running around Hines Park, I'm probably geeking out over science, math, computer science, and artificial intelligence. I started coding in 5th grade with Scratch, making simple games and animations, and eventually moved into JavaScript, Python, machine learning, and AI research. What began as curiosity slowly became something I genuinely love building and learning about every day.

One thing I've noticed is that CS and AI can feel intimidating at first. A lot of students think you need years of experience before you can even start, but having just a little exposure early on can completely change that. Once you build your first project, it starts becoming exciting instead of overwhelming.

That's why I created CodeReach — to make CS and AI education free, approachable, and accessible for every student. With CodeReach, you learn from students who understand your struggles, alongside peers learning with you.

AI is about to change almost everything around us. The question is — are you ready?
